Rebuild Compression

Decompresses and recompresses every already-compressed chunk of the checked group(s) — the raw table plus its hourly / daily / weekly / monthly rollup views. Use this after changing compress segment-by / order-by settings, or after Delete Columns, to apply the new layout to data that was already compressed under the old settings. No row data is touched or lost; only the on-disk storage format is rewritten. An uncompressed chunk (usually the newest one, still inside its compress-after window) is left alone.
